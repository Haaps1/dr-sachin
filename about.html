About Dr Sachin G R

Neurosurgery, refined across continents.

Dr Sachin G R is a renowned senior neurosurgeon trained at PGIMER Chandigarh, with more than 15 years of experience operating on the brain and spine.

He completed skull base fellowship training in Osaka, Japan, and trained in paediatric neurosurgery at SickKids, Toronto. His special interest is the spine, and he specialises in minimally invasive spine surgery, backed by a fellowship in endoscopic spine surgery in South Korea.

His approach is simple: understand the patient fully, try the gentlest effective option first, and when surgery is needed, perform it with precision and care.
